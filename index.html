How to play
  1. 50 cards: 1–10 in five suits. Everyone starts with 4 lives and two tokens, M Major and m Minor.
  2. Each player gets 4 cards. Four more go face up in the pool.
  3. Your four cards make a poker hand. Rankings are below. 1 is low and straights don't wrap.
  4. On your turn, swap one of your cards for a pool card, or pass.
  5. Once everyone has had a turn, you may instead call “Forte!” and place one token face down. Your hand is locked.
  6. Every other player takes one last turn, then places a token face down.
  7. Reveal. M Major means you claim the best hand; m Minor means you claim the worst. Wrong claim: lose a life.
  8. Same kind of hand? Higher values win. A straight goes by its top card; otherwise compare the biggest group first, then the other cards from high to low. An exact tie counts as correct for everyone in it.
  9. At 0 lives you're out. Last player standing wins. If everyone left goes out on the same reveal, the game is a draw.
